beadot converts your photos into fuse bead patterns with accurate color matching.

Just take a photo or pick one from your library, and beadot will generate a precise bead pattern for Perler Beads, Nano Beads, or Hama Beads.

--- KEY FEATURES ---

- Auto-generate bead patterns from any photo
- Perler Beads (57 colors) / Nano Beads (50 colors) / Hama Beads (52 colors)
- CIEDE2000 color difference formula for accurate color matching
- Floyd-Steinberg dithering for smooth gradients
- Square / Hexagonal / Circle / Heart / Star plate shapes
- S / L / 2L / 4L / Custom sizes
- Color / Symbol / Number display modes
- Shopping list (auto-calculated bead counts by color)
- Actual-size PDF export (print and use immediately)
- Gallery to save and manage your patterns
- 100% local processing (data stays on your device)
- Japanese / English / Chinese support
